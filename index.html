03 — ABOUT

Chemist by degree,
TD by obsession

I started in a lab coat — troubleshooting HPLC instruments and co-authoring nanoparticle research — then traded the bench for the viewport. The throughline never changed: I love being the bridge between art and development, diagnosing hard problems and handing the team a solution they can actually use.

Today I'm the Unreal Pipeline TD at WildBrain. The studio's pipeline framework was built by a colleague — I build the production tool layer on top of it: asset & shot importers, crowd VAT baking, light rigs, turntable scripted actions and a light mixer, dropping into C++ whenever Python runs out of road. The tools ship with UE 5.8, wired into ShotGrid, Perforce and the farm.

Before pipelines I shipped realtime: Unreal virtual production at BRON, Netflix-bound 3D specials as a Technical Artist, marketing cinematics for Lords Mobile, and two game projects at Vancouver Film School.
